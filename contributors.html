What counts as a contribution

Code and documentation count, and so do testing, hosting, domain knowledge and time spent walking through real workflows. RPMS itself is the work of the Indian Health Service and the tribal and urban programs that run it. OpenRPMS builds on that work and is not affiliated with or endorsed by IHS.

Join in

Contributions from individuals and organizations are welcome. Start with an issue describing what you would like to add or improve. You can also try RPMS on the demo or run your own.
