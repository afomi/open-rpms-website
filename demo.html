Try RPMS

demo.openrpms.org is a running RPMS system you can sign in to from a browser. It holds the demo clinic: staff you can sign in as, and patients who are made up. Look up a patient, read a chart, and walk through the workflows the clinic's staff use. Before you sign in, please read the terms below.

Open the demo

Terms of use

By signing in to the demo, you agree to these terms. The demo shows the same terms when you sign in.

  • It is a test environment. Every patient in it is synthetic, and it is not used for care.
  • Never enter real patient information. That includes your own, a family member's, or anyone else's. Enter only made-up information.
  • Anyone can sign in. The sign-in pairs are public, so others can see and change what you enter. Expect other visitors' test data in it too.
  • Data may be reset at any time. The demo is put back to its starting data from time to time, and anything entered is erased.
  • No promise of availability. It may be slow, down, or changed without notice.
  • Use it fairly. Don't try to break in, overload it, or reach anything beyond the demo. Report a security problem privately, through the repository's security page, not in a public issue.

Sign in as a demo persona

Each persona is a member of the demo clinic's staff, with the access that role has in RPMS. Signing in as a different persona shows how the same system looks from another job.

Each persona sees only what its role allows. A screen that refuses you is often RPMS working as designed for that job; try the persona who does that work.

Things to try

Each of these is a step in a clinic's day. Try one, and tell us whether it went the way your clinic works.

  1. Find a patient. As any persona, type a name in Find a patient at the top of the page, such as DEMO, and open one of the demo patients.
  2. Register a new patient. As the registration clerk, choose Registration and search for a made-up name first, so nobody gets a second chart. Then register them with a name, sex, date of birth, tribe of membership and community of residence; type the first letters of a tribe or community to choose one. Search again to find them.
  3. Book an appointment. As the scheduler, choose Scheduling, pick a clinic and a day, and book a patient into an open time. The clinic's day then shows the appointment, and the patient's chart lists it.
  4. Read a chart. As the provider, open DEMO,PATIENT ONE and look over the problem list, allergies, vital signs and immunizations. The demo patients have no medications on file yet.
  5. Look from another role. Sign in as a different persona and open the same patient. The chart reads the same for every role, because in RPMS everyone who can sign on to the chart can read it. What changes is the work each role can do: the menu shows Registration to the registration clerk, Scheduling to the scheduler and Referrals to the provider. The nurse and the medical assistant work from the chart alone.

These steps follow the clinic workflows described on this site. If a step is missing, or works differently than you expect, that is exactly the feedback we want.

Tell us what you find

Feedback goes in GitHub issues, where everyone can see it and follow what happens next. You need a free GitHub account to open one.

  • A workflow that doesn't match how your clinic works. Say which job you do, what you were trying to get done, and where the steps went somewhere you didn't expect.
  • Something broken. Say which persona you signed in as, what you clicked or typed, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot helps.
  • An idea. Describe the need first, then the solution you have in mind.
Open an issue
